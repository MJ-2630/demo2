Firework Safety & Guide

Learn how to enjoy fireworks safely and responsibly

Safety First

Always keep a bucket of water nearby and never light fireworks indoors.

Adult Supervision

Children should use fireworks only under adult supervision.

Outdoor Use

Use fireworks in open outdoor spaces away from flammable materials.

Handle with Care

Do not relight malfunctioning fireworks. Dispose safely.
